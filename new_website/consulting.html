Our story

Built in the community, refined at the market.

We have operated successfully as Crêpes de Luxe for over 10 years, starting with community events and farmers' markets in 2004 before opening our West Side Market stand in 2006.

We have refined operations and created menus that are simple to prepare and satisfying to customers. Along the way, we have learned from mistakes and found ways to work more easily and quickly while improving the customer experience and keeping costs in line.

What we can provide

Sound advice for your next stage.

  • Menu planning
  • Training
  • Site selection help
  • Equipment and supply suggestions
  • Resources to open or expand your business

We do not franchise or license our business or name. We offer sound advice at what we believe is a reasonable fee.
